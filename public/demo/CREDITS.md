# Demo photo credits

The listing and category photos under `public/demo/` are real photographs used
only for the Purana Bazaar demo. They were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons,
the WordPress Photo Directory, Flickr, and Rawpixel, then cropped to a square,
resized to 800 × 800, and converted to WebP.

- Listing photos: `listings/` (card thumbnails in `listings/thumbs/`)
- Category fallbacks: `categories/`

The complete, item-by-item attribution — title, creator, licence, and source
link — is available in the app at `/credits` and in
[`src/lib/photo-credits.js`](../../src/lib/photo-credits.js).
